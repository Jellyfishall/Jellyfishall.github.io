I am a PhD student of the RAINZ-CDT programme at The University of Manchester. My aim is to become a research scientist and improve people's living quality by providing high-quality and human-caring human-robot interaction application. My research interest includes Human-Robot Interaction, Cognitive Robotics and Affective Computing. Specificly, my current research focuses on Theory of Mind and Intention Reading in Human-Robot Interaction. Beforehead, I received my MRes degree from The University of Edinburgh, researching Affective Computing in Human-Robot Interaction.
