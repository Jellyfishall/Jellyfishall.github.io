Research Projects

Enhance Interpretability: A Bio-Inspired Affect-Consistent Robot Decision & Behaviour Integrated Generation Model

  • Goal: improve the interpretability of robot decisions by making internal affective dynamics observable through both choices and body behaviours.
  • My contribution: proposed the affect-consistent framework, modelled emotion waves inspired by biological arousal mechanisms, and connected affective states with decision-making and behaviour generation.
  • Implementation: designed and evaluated emotion-aware motion planning and MDP-based decision mechanisms in ROS/Gazebo HRI scenarios.

Researches Advanced in Human-Computer Collaboration and Human-Machine Cooperation: From Variances to Common Prospect

  • Goal: clarify how Human-Computer Collaboration and Human-Machine Cooperation differ, overlap, and converge as intelligent systems become more autonomous.
  • My contribution: reviewed and compared the two research streams from data gathering, decision support, interaction interfaces, and collaborative control perspectives.
  • Outcome: identified shared challenges, including human trust, machine autonomy, interaction transparency, and future directions for human-machine collaborative intelligence.

Course Projects

Autonomous Perception-Driven Exploration & Pick-and-Place Robotic System

  • Goal: Develop an autonomous mobile robotic system capable of executing a complete “sense-plan-act” pipeline in an unknown environment, including SLAM-based exploration, perception-driven object recognition, navigation, and manipulation for colour-matching pick-and-place tasks.
  • My Contribution: Designed and implemented the perception subsystem using depth-camera-based computer vision to recognise target blocks, bins, bin openings, and platforms. Developed object-attribute classification for colours and shapes, and published recognised object coordinates to the integrated ROS2 robotic framework for downstream navigation and manipulation.
  • Implementation: Built a real-time perception pipeline using ROS2, depth sensing, and deep-learning-based object detection/classification. Integrated coordinate transformation and object-pose publishing into the autonomous robotic architecture, enabling robust environment understanding and task execution in simulated and real-world robotic scenarios.

Intelligent Tracking Multi-Function Rover

  • Goal: build a rover capable of color-based tracking, navigation, and task execution with an integrated fish-feeding mechanism.
  • My contribution: architected the software workflow for color-matching navigation, laptop communication, and the embedded control pipeline.
  • Implementation: developed on STM32CubeIDE with STM32F446RE, assembled the mechanical feeding device, and tuned color recognition/tracking to improve accuracy from about 30% to over 90%.
